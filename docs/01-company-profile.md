# 1. Company profile and scope

**Harbor Café Ltd.** (fictional) is a café with 12 staff, one shop, and online orders for pickup.

## Assets in scope

| ID | Asset | Owner | Notes |
|----|-------|-------|-------|
| A01 | Point-of-sale (POS) terminal and card reader | Manager | Takes card payments |
| A02 | Office laptop (Windows) | Owner | Accounting, payroll, supplier invoices |
| A03 | Staff smartphones | Staff | Used for the shared email and the rota |
| A04 | Cloud email and documents (shared account) | Owner | One login used by several staff |
| A05 | Online ordering platform (third-party) | Manager | Customer names, phone numbers, orders |
| A06 | Customer loyalty spreadsheet | Manager | Names, emails, birthdays |
| A07 | Router / firewall from the internet provider | Owner | Default settings |
| A08 | Wi-Fi network (staff + guests on the same network) | Owner | One password, shared with customers |
| A09 | Payroll and HR files | Owner | Staff personal data |
| A10 | Backups (one external USB drive) | Owner | Not tested, kept next to the laptop |

## Out of scope

Physical security of the building, food safety systems and the internet provider's own infrastructure.
